About Us

Abantu Environmental Services (Pty) Ltd is an environmental consultancy firm established in 2018. The company has a country wide footprint and specialises in environmental impact assessments, environmental specialist studies, water use licence applications, and environmental compliance auditing as well as monitoring for all kinds of developments, both inland and coastal.

Our advisory services span all development stages — feasibility, planning and design, construction, operations and decommissioning — for both the public and private sectors, including all government spheres, property development, agriculture and farmers, mining, and energy and electricity.

The company is a Level 1 BBBEE contributor with 100% youth ownership by a sole director who falls under the category of Historically Disadvantaged Individuals (HDI) and hails from rural areas of South Africa. We are registered with the South African Council for Natural Scientific Professions (SACNASP) and the Environmental Assessment Practitioners Association of South Africa (EAPASA).

Our head office is in Keiskammahoek, Amathole District, Eastern Cape, with a presence in Gqeberha and East London (Eastern Cape), Eshowe (KwaZulu-Natal), Plettenberg Bay and Lwandle Strand (Western Cape), Kathu (Northern Cape) and Pretoria (Gauteng).

"Abantu Environmental thrives to contribute to employment creation and skills transfer in environmental consulting, research, GIS, and project management."

Our Values

People

Service Offerings

1. Environmental Licencing

Environmental Impact Assessments (EIAs), Basic Assessments (BAs), Water Use Licence Applications (WULAs), Mining Permit Applications, Borrow Pits Permits, Prospecting and Mining Rights Applications, Atmospheric Emissions Licence Applications.

2. Environmental Compliance Auditing

Environmental Compliance Audits, auditing of landfill sites, waste transfer stations and storage facilities, Environmental Control Officer (ECO) services, Water Use Licence Audits, and any statutory environmental compliance audits.

3. Waste Planning and Management

Integrated Waste Management Plans (IWMPs), municipal waste planning, landfill site operations management, supply of landfill machinery (plant) and equipment, Waste Management Licence Applications, facility registrations with Norms and Standards, and landfill/transfer station planning, design, tender documentation, construction supervision and contract administration.

4. Environmental and Marine Monitoring

Water quality monitoring, air quality monitoring (equipment supply, calibration and maintenance), noise quality compliance investigations, marine water and sediment quality assessments, and marine biodiversity assessments.

5. Environmental Specialist Assessment

Terrestrial biodiversity and plants, animal species, avifaunal surveys, wetlands and riparian delineation, aquatic biodiversity, hydrological, palaeontology, archaeological and cultural heritage, and GIS and remote sensing applications.
